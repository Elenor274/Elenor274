## About me

I'm a software engineer at **GolPakhsheAval (Golrang Industrial Group)**. I also develop business software through **Sepehr Pardaz**, my own software company, with a focus on Python, Django, and PostgreSQL.

My current work centers on ERP and manufacturing systems: turning real operational workflows into software that is traceable, maintainable, and safe to update without disrupting business data.

- Backend development with **Python, Django, and PostgreSQL**
- Manufacturing workflows: orders, product variants, BOM, planning, production, and inventory
- Data integrity, audit trails, reporting, testing, and safe database migrations
- Practical Windows deployment for server and network-client environments
- Based in Iran

## Current focus

### HAMA Manufacturing ERP — private client project

A production ERP developed by Sepehr Pardaz for managing customer orders, color-based product variants, BOM, production planning, operator performance, warehouse operations, and end-to-end traceability.

The project emphasizes reliable multi-user operation, controlled updates, historical-data preservation, and practical factory use.
